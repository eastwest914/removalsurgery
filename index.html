Frequently Asked Questions

What is breast augmentation removal surgery in NYC?

It is a procedure to take out existing breast implants, sometimes along with the scar tissue around them. Some patients choose removal alone, while others combine it with a lift or replace the implants. Your surgeon will recommend the best option after an exam.

Why do women choose breast implant removal?

Common reasons include implant age, capsular contracture, rippling, shifting, pain, a change in personal preference, or a wish for a more natural look. Whatever your reason, we will discuss it openly and without pressure.

How long is recovery after implant removal?

Recovery varies by patient and by the type of procedure. Many people return to light daily activity within about a week, while strenuous exercise usually waits longer. Your surgeon will give you a timeline suited to your surgery.

Will my breasts look different after the implants are removed?

Your breasts may look smaller or softer, and the skin may change depending on implant size and how long they were in place. A breast lift at the same time can improve shape when needed. We review realistic expectations before surgery.
